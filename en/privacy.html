Hello, I’m Argiris Karalis, an IT Infrastructure Specialist & Systems Administrator based in Athens, Greece. With over 20 years of experience in the IT sector, I have developed a deep expertise in managing complex technical environments and delivering reliable solutions.



As a graduate Industrial Informatics Engineer, I combine a solid academic background with extensive hands-on experience. Having worked with major Internet & Telephony Service Providers, I have become an expert in troubleshooting advanced connectivity (FTTH/VDSL/VoIP), IPTV, and enterprise-level network services for both residential and SOHO environments.

Beyond my corporate roles, I have spent over a decade as a Freelance IT Engineer, specializing in Microsoft 365 administration and providing tailored hardware and software support to small businesses and home offices. My journey has shaped me into a versatile professional — a multitasking team player who excels under pressure and is always ready to mentor others or take on the next big technical challenge.
